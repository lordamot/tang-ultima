can we avoid mcu flash rewrites?

********************************************************************************

Yes. The practical approach is to preload a compressed core library into MCU flash once, then only read it during switches.

I checked the current binaries: all six together compress to about 1.87 MiB with zlib, fitting within the 3 MiB after address 0x100000. Stage 2 could decompress the selected core in small RAM buffers while loading it over JTAG. Flash writes would happen only when installing or updating that library.

We must also move the diagnostic log to RAM to eliminate all switch-related MCU flash writes.

Direct SD-to-JTAG streaming cannot work through the current link: erasing the FPGA destroys the UART bridge carrying the data. A complete raw core is 907,418 bytes, exceeding the current stage-2 RAM layout.

The compressed library is feasible from the sizes and code, but needs implementation and board validation.
