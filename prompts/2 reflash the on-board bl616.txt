Prepared, on the host; nothing flashed.  The board is on the bus right now
as 0403:6010 SIPEED "20K's FRIEND", serial 2023030621 - the factory
firmware, and an early board.

Upstream (MiSTle-Dev/FPGA-Companion, successor of the MiSTeryNano firmware)
ships the factory friend_20k image in a plain and an encrypted variant, and
Sipeed's bl616_fpga_partner_20kNano.bin - the FT2232 emulation that also
starts a second firmware at flash 0x40000 when no PC is attached.  That
Partner is the "ftdi" target.  Catch: it is encrypted and runs only on a
chip whose flash-encryption efuse Sipeed set (boards from ~2024); on an
unfused chip it never starts and a custom firmware and the PC-side
programmer are mutually exclusive.  Upstream says the fuse cannot be read
beforehand; it can - ef_sf_aes_mode, efuse word 0 bits [1:0] in the SDK's
ef_data_reg.h, and BLFlashCommand --efuse --read.

Makefile: onboard-status, onboard-fetch (bin/onboard/, sha256 pinned to
commit 49ebb11 / release v1.4.29), onboard-backup (first 1 MB of the chip
-> bin/onboard/backup/), onboard-efuse (decoded by tools/efuse_bl616.py),
flash-mcu-onboard-orig / -orig-encrypted / -ftdi / -stage2 (STAGE2=<file>
at 0x40000) / -restore BACKUP=<file>.  tools/onboard.sh refuses a write
unless exactly one BL616 is in boot mode and it cannot be the dock; every
image is checked for the BFNP/FCFG header first.

Recovery, in .claude/docs/onboard.md: the ISP is in the BL616's mask ROM
and UPDATE-at-power-up always reaches it; nothing writes efuses; the FPGA
boots from its own flash whatever the BL616 holds.  Stays "Bouffalo CDC
DEMO" after a flash = wrong variant, flash the backup or the other
variant.  Chip really dead = program the FPGA through TP3..TP6 with any
openFPGALoader cable; the OSD install from the card still works.

Order: onboard-status (seen), onboard-backup, onboard-efuse, then ftdi
only if fused.  Checked here: the targets dry-run, fetch works, status
works, the refusals fire, the decoder decodes; the flash reads and writes
are untried on the board.  Docs: onboard.md new; CLAUDE.md, build.md,
handover.md, progress.md, CHANGELOG.md touched.  Not committed.
